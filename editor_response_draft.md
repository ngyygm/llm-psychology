Dear Editors Da San Martino, Jiang, Wan, White, and Zhu, and Program Committee Chairs Dev, Elliott, Lee, Li, Purwarianti, Suzuki, Tong, and Xu,

Thank you for your message of [DATE]. I am writing to acknowledge the desk rejection and to take full responsibility for the citation-integrity failures you identified.

On receiving your decision, I independently re-verified each of the nine flagged references and then conducted a complete audit of the entire bibliography. I confirm that the problems are real, and I want to disclose honestly that my own audit found them to be **more extensive than the nine citations you flagged**.

The submitted bibliography was generated by an automated literature-review pipeline, and I did not manually verify its output against primary sources before submission. The result was that real papers were paired with fabricated author lists, and a small number of entirely fictitious entries were introduced. This was a failure of my oversight; the use of the tool does not lessen my responsibility for it.

The full scope I found, across roughly 70 cited references:
- **Three entirely fabricated entries** — no such paper exists (the items flagged as Schaeckermann et al., Hagendorff "Machine Personality Traits," and Serpell & Ney).
- **Eight real papers attributed to fabricated or wrong authors** (including the Acerbi & Stubbersfield entry, which duplicated the real Salecha & Ireland paper under a second, invented author list — the Salecha & Ireland attribution itself is correct and has been retained).
- **Approximately eight further entries with incorrect metadata** — wrong arXiv identifiers, years, given names, or venues.

All nine citations you identified fall within this defective set.

Corrective actions I have taken or committed to:
1. **Full re-verification.** I have re-checked every reference in the bibliography against canonical sources (DBLP, arXiv, the ACL Anthology, and DOI registries) and produced a corrected bibliography.
2. **Removal of phantom citations.** The three fabricated entries have been removed, and all misattributed authors and incorrect metadata have been corrected.
3. **Process change.** I will no longer use automated reference generation without manual, entry-by-entry verification against primary indices, and I will run a citation audit before any future submission of this or related work.

I accept the desk rejection and am not requesting reconsideration. I also accept the referral to the Publication Ethics Committee and will cooperate fully and promptly with any request for information, documentation, or clarification. I am glad to share my full citation-audit report and corrected bibliography with the committee should that be useful.

I sincerely apologize to you, the Area Chair, and the program committee for the extra work this has caused and for falling short of the standards the venue and our field expect.
